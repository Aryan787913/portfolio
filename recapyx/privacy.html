Recapyx privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026 · Home

In short

Recapyx turns the captions Google Meet already shows on your screen into notes. It keeps everything on your device. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. It never records audio.

What it reads

  • Meet captions on meet.google.com tabs, after captions are on: speaker name, caption text and time.
  • The meeting tab's picture, only when you press the screenshot button or shortcut.
  • Your name, if you type it, to replace “You” in your notes.
  • Your Groq API key, if you paste one.

Where it is stored

On this device only: transcripts, highlights, screenshots and summaries in the extension's own IndexedDB; settings and your Groq key in the extension's local storage (chrome.storage.local). Nothing is stored on the meeting page. Removing the extension deletes all of it. You can delete any meeting from the dashboard or the meeting page.

What leaves your device

Only one thing, and only when you ask: if you saved your own Groq key and click Summarise, Ask, What did I miss? or Test key, the extension sends the needed part of that transcript (or, for Test key, just the key) to Groq's API at api.groq.com, using your key. Groq's own terms and privacy policy apply to that request. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

The optional chat notice is a message you post into the meeting chat yourself (or that is posted only if you turn on “always post”), so others know notes are being taken.

What it does not do

  • No audio or video recording; no bot joins your call.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of your data; no use for advertising, credit or lending decisions; no human reads your data.
  • No remote code: everything the extension runs is inside the extension package.

Limited Use

The use of information received from the browser and its tabs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's single purpose: notes from Google Meet captions, kept on your device.
