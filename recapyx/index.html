Recapyx

Free Google Meet transcript & AI meeting notes

Free Google Meet transcript and AI notes, without a bot

Recapyx is a Chrome extension that turns Google Meet's live captions into a clean transcript with speaker names, then gives you an AI meeting summary with decisions and action items. Nothing joins your call, no audio is recorded, and your notes stay in your browser.

Add to Chrome, it's free

Features

  • Live Google Meet transcript with speaker names and timestamps
  • Highlights and slide screenshots with one shortcut
  • AI meeting summary: decisions, action items with owner and due date, open questions
  • Student mode for online classes: key points, revision notes, likely exam questions
  • "What did I miss?" recap of the last 10 minutes
  • Export meeting notes as TXT, Markdown or PDF
  • Search all your past meetings
  • English summaries from Hindi and Hinglish captions

How it works

  1. Install Recapyx and join a Google Meet call.
  2. Turn on captions (CC). Notes start on their own.
  3. When the call ends, get your summary and export your notes.

Private by design

Transcripts, screenshots and settings are stored only on your device. AI summaries use your own free Groq API key and run only when you click. No account, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

FAQ

Is it free? Yes. The transcript, highlights, screenshots and export are free. AI summaries use a free Groq key you add yourself.

Does it record audio? No. It only reads the captions Google Meet already shows on your screen.

Does it work with Zoom or Microsoft Teams? Not yet. Google Meet only for now.
