Flood Triage

Ranking citizen flood photos for ward officers

Question
During urban flooding, which citizen street photos show the worst water levels, so officers can respond to them first?
Data
STURM-FloodDepth (CC BY 4.0): 340 held-out test crops of flooded vehicles, plus 20 real Indian monsoon photos for a qualitative check.
Method
Meta's open-weight Muse Spark Open, prompted few-shot, reads the water level against the vehicle (L0–L4) or answers "can't tell". It's combined with a CLIP linear-probe baseline. A Gradio app ranks reports worst-first and drafts a ward note.
Features
  • Reads the water level against vehicle parts (tyre, chassis, window: L0–L4), or says "can't tell" for blurry or vehicle-less photos
  • Shows its evidence: an overlay with the vehicle box and the waterline
  • Checks the citizen's own depth guess against the photo
  • Flags likely duplicate reports
  • Ranks the queue worst-first
  • Drafts a short note for the ward officer
Result
0.633Macro-F1 on the leak-free test split, against 0.580 for the baseline. Gross errors (off by 2+ levels) dropped from 35 to 17. The test set was scored once.

Terms Desk

Should this invoice get an early-payment discount?

Question
For each PayPal invoice, does an early-payment discount actually earn cash, or does it just give margin to clients who'd pay on time anyway?
Data
Payment Date Dataset (CC0, Kaggle): 41,555 closed B2B invoices. Trained on 2019 and tested on 2020, with no leakage.
Method
An XGBoost model predicts the chance each client pays on time. A rule-based expected-value check blocks any discount that loses money, and nothing is sent until a human approves. It acts through the PayPal Invoicing v2 API (sandbox).
Features
  • Predict: the chance each client pays on time, from their invoice history
  • Skeptic: a deterministic expected-value check that blocks any money-losing discount
  • Approval queue: nothing is sent until you click approve
  • Act: creates and sends the PayPal invoice with the discount
  • Enforce: if the invoice is unpaid when the window closes, it resets to full price
  • Ledger: logs every blocked discount and the money it saved
Result
0.783ROC AUC on the 2020 test set, against 0.749 for a client-history baseline. One verified end-to-end PayPal sandbox run went from invoice to discounted payment.
